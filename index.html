Tulips, windmills and canals

Four nights in Leiden, three in Amsterdam. Trains, buses, bikes and boats throughout.

  4. 14:30Check in, drop bags

  6. EveningCanal walk through the old town, early dinner

Before you book

  • UK ETA for both, well before travel. Passports valid 6 months past 1 June 2027.
  • ETIAS for the Netherlands and Spain, expected live in 2027, €20, over-70s exempt.
  • Keukenhof tickets + bus 854 + boat tour, the day they release.
  • Tulip cruise Flagship Amsterdam, afternoon of 5 May.
  • Van Gogh Museum 09:00 slot on 8 May, online only.
  • Giethoorn train or coach tour, then the whisper boat.
  • Highlands tour 3-day Skye tour before any Scottish hotel.
  • Sagrada Família plus Park Güell and Casa Batlló.
  • Premier Suites Redcliffe about 17 nights across four blocks — ask for one rate.
  • Travel insurance the whole 29 Apr – 1 Jun window.
